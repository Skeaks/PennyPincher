PennyPincher · closed pilot

The price you see is not the price. It is one of several.

Retailers run price experiments: the same item, the same store, the same minute, different prices for different shoppers. No one shopper can see that. A panel can. PennyPincher is a browser extension and a consented panel that, together, show you where the price you were shown sits.

The panel

The extension records the price your browser already drew on the page while you shop on a supported retailer. It does not sign in, click, search, or navigate for you. It reads what you were shown, and nothing else.

Every panelist's records are pooled on our server under a random ID that changes every 7 days. For each product, store, and delivery option, the pool is turned into a ladder: the distinct prices shoppers were shown, how many saw each one, and where yours sits. You see the ladder in the extension's popup, with the number of observers it rests on.

The lever probe

Some questions do not need a panel. Is being signed in changing your price right now? The extension asks the retailer for the public page of the product you are viewing, the way a visitor who is not signed in would see it, without your cookies or sign-in, at most once an hour per product. Then it puts the two prices side by side. That works on day one, with no other panelists at all.

When we do not know, we say so

A ladder built on too few observers is a guess, and a guess presented as a floor is worse than no answer. When the panel has not seen enough of a product to be sure, the popup says UNRESOLVED, shows how many more observers it needs, and shows nothing else. No estimate is ever shown without the number of observers behind it, and no number on this site or in the extension is a promise about what you will pay.

What it never does

  • Never reads your retailer password, sign-in session, cookies, or payment details.
  • Never records your name, email, phone number, street address, full ZIP code, IP address, or browser fingerprint.
  • Never records anything on sites other than the supported retailers, or on pages that are not product, search, aisle, or storefront pages.
  • Never sends anything until you have read and agreed to a consent screen that lists every field and every request.
