# S11 outcome, PR 1 of 2 (API half), 2026-09-07

Shipped: `GET /v1/cells/:cellKey` in `apps/api`. Reads the cell's last 72 h through a new
`ObservationRepo.listByCell` (D1 on the `(cell_key, observed_at)` index, Map in tests),
collapses the rows to one vote per panelist (latest `observedAt`; ties on `receivedAt`, then
`observationId`), runs `resolve()` over the votes and returns `{ cellKey, resolution, n,
panelists, observations, latestObservedAt, updatedAt, summary, windowHours }`. Same bearer
check as ingest, factored into one `denied()` helper. Cached at the edge via `caches.default`
with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=60`, keyed on the URL after the bearer check. 19 tests,
no network; the brief's synth case (stickiness 1, 10 observers, 200 draws) reports `n: 10`,
`observations: 200`, `UNRESOLVED / insufficient_n`.

What the brief got wrong: `owns:` lists only `routes/cells.ts` and `cells.test.ts`, but the
route cannot exist without touching `app.ts` (mount, shared bearer check), the repo interface
and both implementations (`listByCell`), `index.ts` (the cache binding) and `package.json`
(stats as a dependency, synth for the test). All inside `apps/api`; the extension half is
untouched. Also, `n` and `panelists` are the same number by construction once the dedupe
runs, so the response carries `observations` (rows before the collapse) as the count that
actually differs; without it a client cannot tell that dedupe happened. The brief's ten-observer
case needs a five-tier ladder: with three tiers, ten independent votes can clear the coupon
threshold (9) and resolve, which is correct behaviour but not the `insufficient_n` the brief
asks for.

What was harder than expected: nothing in the endpoint itself. Two small traps. `ObservationRow`
is flat (`priceMinor`, `currency`) while the resolver wants `facts.price`, so the route maps
rows to `ObservationLike` explicitly. And ISO timestamps are only lexicographically
chronological within one precision ("...:18Z" sorts after "...:18.000Z"), so the D1 range
query widens its bounds by a second and the route re-filters exactly.

Not done, deliberately: the extension half (sync alarm, ladder popup) waits for S17 (#26) to
merge, per the brief; it is PR 2 and branches from `main` after that. `facts.isEstimate`
observations are still not filtered (S09 flag stands). No row limit on the cell query; a pilot
cell will not exceed a few hundred rows in 72 h, and S14 owns ingest limits.
