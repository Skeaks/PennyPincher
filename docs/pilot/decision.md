## The rule

From `docs/sessions/S16-closed-pilot.md`, thresholds in `pilot.json`:

- If >= 20% of RESOLVED cells show multiple tiers: Track B is real. Phase 2 briefs are the
  basket optimizer and the receipt (observed floor vs price paid, as an interval with N).
- If the lever probe finds logged-in vs logged-out differences on >= 10% of comparable checks
  but cells are single-tier: Track A is the product. Phase 2 briefs are the ZIP / fulfilment
  probes and the subscription.
- If neither: the pivot. Phase 2 briefs are "one-price certified" and the conventional levers
  from the research doc.
- Minimums (this session's addition, Jamie's to keep or change): 20 RESOLVED cells before the
  multi-tier share counts as evidence, 50 comparable probe checks before the difference rate
  does. Below either, the verdict is INCONCLUSIVE and the pilot runs on. A share of three
  cells is a coin flip, and the brief's rule did not say what to do with one.

Definitions the tool applies:

- A cell is the pilot retailer's `retailer|store|sku|fulfillment|zip3`, resolved over the
  whole pilot window with one vote per panelist and abuse suspects excluded, by `resolve()`
  from `packages/stats`: the API's rule with a longer window.
- A probe check is an anonymous row (`logged_out`, `cleanSession`) paired with the same
  panelist's logged-in row for the same SKU within the hour before it. It is comparable when
  both sides resolved to one store under the extension's own rule (`compare.ts`; Instacart
  needs a location id on both sides). Differences are MORE and LESS verdicts over comparable
  checks, every retailer counted.
